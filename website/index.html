14 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

COMMON
QUESTIONS.

How do I get a price from A Team?

Use the instant estimator at ateamcontractings.com/estimate. Answer a few questions, snap a photo of the area you need cleaned, and you'll see a ballpark price right away. No callback required. Or text Anthony directly at (937) 777-9093 and he'll get right back to you.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Tipp City, Ohio and serve the Miami Valley and greater Dayton: Troy, Vandalia, Huber Heights, Englewood, Fairborn, Beavercreek, Piqua, and the towns around them. If you're not sure whether we come to you, just ask.

What's the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?

Pressure washing uses high-force water to blast concrete, driveways, patios, and sidewalks clean. Soft washing uses low-pressure water plus a biodegradable cleaning solution to safely clean siding, roofs, and delicate surfaces. Using the wrong method can cause real damage, so we use the right one for every surface. Learn more about soft washing →

Do I need to be home during the job?

Not necessarily. As long as we have access to a water spigot and the areas that need cleaning, we can complete most jobs while you're at work or out for the day. We'll send you before-and-after photos when we're done and check in before we leave.

Are you insured?

Yes. A Team Contracting is fully insured. You can ask for proof of insurance any time, and we're happy to provide it before the job starts.

How quickly can you get out here?

Usually within a few days. In our busy season (spring and summer) we book up fast, so the sooner you reach out the better. Call or text (937) 777-9093 and we'll get you on the schedule.

Do you offer discounts for bundling services?

Yes. Bundling is the best deal we offer. Add window cleaning to a pressure wash or soft wash while we're already on site and you'll save vs. booking each separately. We'll show you the full value and your A Team discount when we give you the price.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, check, Venmo, Zelle, and card. Payment is due when the job is complete and you've done the walkthrough and are happy with the results. No payment upfront required.
