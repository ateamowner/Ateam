Local Knowledge

The MetroPark Side Of Town Stays Wet Longer

Englewood MetroPark and the Stillwater sit on the east edge of town. The walls that face that direction do not dry the way a west wall on National Road dries.

What grows there is a living organism with roots in the pores of the siding. Blast the top off with a pressure washer and the wall is clean that afternoon and green again by spring. Soft washing soaks, dwells, and kills it. The water is only there to carry the solution on and take it away again.

The west and south walls that face US-40 have a different problem — road film on top of whatever biology is there. Same visit, same crew, different chemistry. We do not treat every elevation like it is the same house.
