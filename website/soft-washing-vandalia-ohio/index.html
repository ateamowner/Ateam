The Thing Nobody Mentions

A Pressure Washer Can Void Your Siding Warranty

If your house is newer than about 2015, read the siding warranty before anyone points a machine at it. Most vinyl warranties carve out damage caused by high-pressure water.

That is not fine print for the sake of it. A pressure wand drives water up under the lap of the panel, where it sits in the wall cavity with no way out. On a new build with housewrap behind it, that is exactly where you do not want standing water. The siding also marks: concentrated spray on a warm afternoon can leave a permanent dull patch that no amount of cleaning brings back.

Soft washing runs at roughly the pressure of a garden hose. The detergent does the cleaning and the water only rinses. It is what the siding manufacturers actually recommend, and it is the difference between a clean house and a claim your builder will not honor.

Local Knowledge

Why Vandalia Grows It Quicker Than You'd Expect

You are on low ground between two rivers

Vandalia sits on the valley floor between the Great Miami and the Stillwater. Low ground between two rivers holds damp air far longer than the higher ground either side of it, and damp air is half of what algae needs — shade is the other half. Same siding, same age, same paint: a Vandalia house weathers faster than one on a ridge ten miles away, and homeowners here often assume they got a bad batch of siding when what they actually have is geography.

The new developments have no tree cover yet

There is a lot of new build going up around here — the Riverdale development off South Brown School Road, and more near the airport. Those streets get full sun and open sky, which people assume protects them. It helps the south wall. The north wall still never sees direct sun, and with nothing but young saplings on the lots there is no canopy to slow the moisture down either. On a new estate the north elevation usually starts showing somewhere around year three, and the whole street shows at once because the houses went up at once.

Identical houses means identical problems, which is good news

On a street where every house is the same age, the same siding and the same orientation, the job is predictable. We can quote a whole cul-de-sac off one walk-around, and if a few neighbors want the same week we can price it better than doing them one at a time. It is the single easiest way to get a Vandalia house washed cheaply.
