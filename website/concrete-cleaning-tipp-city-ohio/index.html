🚗

Driveways

The biggest piece of concrete most people own and the first thing anyone sees. Algae, tire marks, and years of grey buildup come off to an even finish.

Sidewalks & Walkways

The path to your front door sets the tone for the whole house. Clean walkways also stop being slick once the algae is gone.

Patios & Pool Decks

Poured concrete, stamped, and exposed aggregate all clean up — at pressures chosen for the surface so the finish and any coating survive.

How It Works

Simple. Honest. Done Right.

No mystery. Here's exactly what happens when you call A-Team:

1

You call or text — we pick up.

Rough dimensions of the driveway or patio are usually enough for a straight quote.

2

We identify what's actually staining it.

Algae, oil, rust, and general grey all respond to different things. Treating them the same is why some jobs come out blotchy.

3

We pre-treat before any water.

Detergent goes down first and gets dwell time. This is the step that separates a clean surface from a wet one.

4

We run a surface cleaner, not a wand.

A spinning surface cleaner covers evenly at a consistent distance. That's what prevents the striped, zebra-print look.

5

We detail the edges and rinse.

Edges, expansion joints, and corners get finished by hand, then the whole slab gets rinsed and you walk it with us.

What Drives the Price of Concrete Cleaning

Square footage is the starting point, not the whole story. Send a photo through the estimator and we'll price your actual slab.

Square footage

The main driver. A two-car driveway and a long ribbon drive are very different amounts of surface.

What's on it

General algae and grey come off predictably. Oil, rust, and paint need targeted pre-treatment and more time.

Surface type

Broom-finished concrete, stamped, exposed aggregate, and pavers each take different pressure and technique.

How long since the last clean

A slab that's never been cleaned takes more passes than one done every couple of seasons.

Access and water

We need a reachable outdoor spigot. Unusually long runs or awkward access add setup time.

Bundling

Driveway, walkway, and patio together in one visit costs less than booking each on its own.
