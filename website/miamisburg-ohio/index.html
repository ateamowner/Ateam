Local Knowledge

River Town Brick and Newer Subdivisions

Miamisburg runs along the Great Miami River, and that river valley keeps the air damp through the summer. Damp air and shade are what algae needs, so north walls, shaded patios and the concrete beside downspouts green up first, like the patio in the photo above.

The older streets near downtown have brick and painted wood that should never take full pressure, while the newer neighborhoods on the edges of town are mostly vinyl with poured concrete patios and drives. Brick, wood and vinyl get a low-pressure soft wash. Concrete gets the surface cleaner. One visit can do both.

Nearby we also work Springboro, Centerville, Kettering and Dayton.
