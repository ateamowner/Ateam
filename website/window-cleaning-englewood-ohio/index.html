Local Knowledge

The Glass Faces The Same Road The Siding Does

Windows on National Road collect the same oily film the siding does — plus the mineral haze this water leaves if you rinse with a hose.

Everything here dries with hard water on it

Water in this part of the Miami Valley comes up through limestone, loaded with calcium and magnesium. Wash a window with it and the water goes but the minerals stay, as the white haze people read as still dirty. South-facing panes come out worst because they dry fastest — the water is gone before it can run off, so everything it was carrying is left on the glass.

Exterior glass gets rinsed with purified water that has had those minerals stripped out. It dries leaving nothing, which is why we do not towel the outside. That is also why a hose rinse between visits can leave a pane looking worse than before you started.

The road side of the glass has a film, not a haze

US-40 has been the National Road through here since 1926, and I-70 meets OH-48 inside the city limits. Glass facing that traffic picks up the same fine oily layer the siding does — tire rubber and brake dust rather than minerals. It does not come off with water alone at any temperature, and it is the reason a window can be streak-free and still look dull from the kerb.

Those elevations get detergent and a proper strip before the purified rinse. The park-facing side of the same house usually does not need it. Same visit, different work on different faces.

A lot of this glass is the same age

Englewood grew better than 8% a year through the 1950s and nearly 18% a year through the 1960s, going from a village of about 600 in 1940 to city status in 1971. A good deal of the original glazing from that build-out is still in service, much of it with storm windows over it. Storms double the surfaces that need doing and hold grit in the bottom channel, and the track is where the dirt actually lives — leave it and the pane looks dirty again within a fortnight. Screens and tracks get done in the same visit for that reason, not as an upsell.
