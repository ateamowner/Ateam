Local Knowledge

Newer Two-Story Vinyl, Done Without a Wand

Springboro grew fast, and a big share of its homes are two-story vinyl houses in subdivisions on both sides of State Route 741. Tall vinyl walls are exactly where a pressure washer does damage: water gets forced up under the laps, and the top of the wall is out of reach of a safe wand anyway. A soft wash reaches the second story at low pressure and lets the detergent do the work, like the job in the photo above.

Backyards here tend to be open on one side and shaded by a fence or trees on the other, so the algae shows up on the wall that faces the shade. The patio and back steps next to that wall usually green up too, which is why a lot of Springboro jobs pair a house wash with a patio or driveway.

Springboro sits mostly in Warren County, with part of the city in Montgomery County. Nearby we also work Miamisburg, Centerville, Kettering and Dayton.
