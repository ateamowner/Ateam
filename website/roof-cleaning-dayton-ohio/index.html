What We Clean on a Roof

Different roofs and different growth need different handling. Here's what we take on and how we approach each one.

🏠

Asphalt Shingle Soft Wash

The most common roof around Dayton and the one most easily ruined by high pressure. We apply low-pressure solution, let it work, and rinse gently.

⬛

Black Streaks

Those dark vertical runs are Gloeocapsa magma, an algae that feeds on the limestone filler in shingles. Scrubbing won't fix it — it has to be killed at the source.

🌿

Moss & Lichen

Moss holds water against your shingles and lichen roots into the surface. Both need treatment and patience rather than force, or you pull granules off with them.

🪟

Metal Roofs & Panels

Metal panels and standing seam take a different solution and a gentler touch to avoid stripping finish or spotting the coating.

🍂

Roofline, Eaves & Soffits

The band where roof meets gutter collects the worst of it. Cleaning the roof and that whole edge together is what makes the difference visible from the street.

🌱

Landscaping Protection

We wet down and cover plantings before anything goes on the roof, and rinse the surrounding beds after. Protecting the yard is part of the job, not an afterthought.

What Drives the Price of a Roof Cleaning

Send a photo and I'll give you a straight roof-cleaning price before we start. Where yours lands depends on the factors below — no invented starting number.

Roof size and pitch

Square footage matters, but so does steepness — a steep roof takes longer to treat safely and carefully.

How much growth is on it

Light streaking on a five-year-old roof is a very different job than a north face carpeted in moss.

Roof material

Asphalt shingle, metal, and tile each need a different approach, solution strength, and rinse method.

Height and access

Single story with clear perimeter access is straightforward. Two-story with tight landscaping takes more setup.

Landscaping around the house

Heavy plantings right against the foundation need more protection time before and more rinsing after.

Bundling with other services

Roof, gutters, and siding on the same visit costs less than three separate trips out to your property.
