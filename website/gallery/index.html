Questions About Our Work

Real answers — no fluff.

Pressure washing uses high-force water to blast off tough grime from hard surfaces like concrete, brick, and stone. Soft washing uses low pressure plus cleaning solutions to safely remove mold, algae, and buildup from siding, roofs, and other delicate surfaces. We use whichever method is right for the surface — no one-size-fits-all here.

Most residential driveways and walkways take 1–2 hours. Full house soft washes run 2–4 hours depending on size. Window cleaning jobs are typically under 2 hours for most homes. We move fast but never cut corners.

For exterior-only work (driveways, siding, walkways), you don't need to be home — just make sure we can access the area. For interior window cleaning, yes. Either way, I'll text you before and after so you're never in the dark.

We always pre-wet plants and bushes before starting and rinse them after. Soft washing solutions are diluted to be safe for landscaping when applied correctly — and we've been doing this long enough to know how to protect what matters.

Yes — we serve the entire Dayton metro area including Centerville, Huber Heights, Beavercreek, Fairborn, Troy, Vandalia, Springboro, and Miamisburg. If you're unsure if we come to your area, just call or text.
