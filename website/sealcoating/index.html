What This Page Is

Not a Thin Extra Site. Two Real Jobs.

People say “sealcoat” and mean two different things. We do both. We will not pretend they are the same bucket.

1. Driveway and asphalt sealcoating

The pad in front of the house. Oil spots, winter wear, UV. The asphalt gets cleaned first — that is pressure / power washing — then, when it is dry, we apply sealcoat so you are not painting over algae.

2. Roof clear-sealcoat, bundled with a roof wash

After a roof cleaning, a clear sealcoat on the shingles is what adds 5–10 years. It is not a driveway product on a roof. It is not a substitute for washing. Coat over black streaks and you sealed in the organism.
