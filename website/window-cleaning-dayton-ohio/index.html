What You Get

What a Dayton Window Cleaning Includes

Window cleaning is its own service for us, not something we rush through at the end of a house wash. Here's what a standard Dayton job covers:

  • Exterior glass, first floor and upstairs. The second story is part of the job.
  • Sliding doors and French doors, same as the windows.
  • Screens, taken out and cleaned separately so they stop blocking light.
  • Tracks and frames, wiped out so the windows slide and seal.
  • Purified-water finish, so the glass dries clear instead of spotted.
  • A walkthrough before we leave. If something isn't right, we fix it on the spot.

Inside glass is only done when you ask. Tell us when you book. For inside work, someone needs to be home. For exterior-only jobs you don't need to be home at all, and we'll send before and after photos when we're done.

One thing we never do: put a pressure washer on glass. Windows get window tools. If you want the why, it's in our post on fall window cleaning in Dayton.

Local Knowledge

Dayton Windows Aren't All the Same Job

A house in Belmont or South Park is a different job than a newer two-story out in Kettering or Centerville. In the older city neighborhoods, including the Oregon District and the streets around the University of Dayton, we often find original wood sash, storm windows over the main glass, and storm tracks packed with grit and dead bugs. Clean the glass and skip the track, and the first rain washes that grit right back down the pane.

The suburban two-stories have their own thing: big upstairs windows over a porch roof or a drop-off that most people can't safely reach. That second-story glass is usually the dirtiest on the house, because nobody has touched it since they moved in.

That's why we ask what you've got before we price it, instead of counting windows from the curb. Single or two-story, roughly how many windows, and whether there are storms. A photo usually answers all three.

Hard water is the other Dayton factor. Our water is hard, so glass that gets rinsed with the hose or hit by sprinklers dries spotted. The full story on that is in our fall window cleaning guide.

What moves your number:

  • Heavy hard water buildup. Light spots come off with a normal cleaning. Years of sprinkler spots can take a special treatment, and we'll tell you what will and won't come off before we start.
