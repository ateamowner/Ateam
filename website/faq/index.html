Pressure washing uses high-force water to blast concrete, driveways, patios, and sidewalks clean. People also call that power washing. Soft washing uses low-pressure water plus a biodegradable cleaning solution to safely clean siding, roofs, and delicate surfaces. Using the wrong method can cause real damage — we use the right one for every surface. Concrete is never a soft wash. Soft wash vs pressure wash has the side-by-side, and soft washing is the siding and roof service.

No. Roofs are soft washed. High pressure strips the granules off asphalt shingles, and those granules are what protect the shingle. The roof looks clean the same afternoon and then ages early. Low-pressure soft washing is the method shingle manufacturers generally point to. If your roof is under warranty, check your specific manufacturer's language — we will tell you exactly what we use.
