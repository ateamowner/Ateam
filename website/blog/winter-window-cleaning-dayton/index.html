When we will and won't clean windows in winter

The rule is simple. We clean windows year-round as long as it isn't freezing outside. We don't clean glass in freezing temps.

Why windows still get dirty in winter

Dirty glass doesn't take the season off. Wet streets throw road film onto first-floor panes. The sun sits low, so it hits the glass at an angle and shows every streak, same as it does in late fall. And this is when you look at your windows more: holidays, guests, or a listing photo on a clear, cold day.

If you missed the fall cleaning, winter is a fine time to catch up on a day that isn't frozen. Spring and fall are still the two best times to clean windows in the Dayton area. That timing is in our post on fall window cleaning in Dayton. This post is about the days after that, when you still want clear glass and the thermometer cooperates.

Don't put a pressure washer on the glass

A pressure washer is the wrong tool for windows in any season. Glass gets window tools and a purified-water finish, never a pressure tip. Screens and tracks too. Siding and roofs get a soft wash, low pressure. Driveways and sidewalks get a pressure wash / concrete wash.

What a winter window cleaning includes

It's the same job we do the rest of the year. Exterior glass, first floor and upstairs, plus sliding doors, screens and tracks, and a walkthrough before we leave. The full list lives on our window cleaning in Dayton page.

Standard service is outside only. Inside glass is available when you request it, just tell us when you book. For exterior-only jobs you don't need to be home, and we'll send before and after photos when we're done.

If we're already at your place for a house wash in Dayton or gutter cleaning in Dayton, mention the windows when you ask for your price. A house wash before the windows means the runoff from the siding doesn't land on clean glass.

Realtors, storefronts and property managers

If a house is going on the market this winter, get the windows done before the photographer comes, not after. Dirty glass shows up in listing photos, especially on a sunny front shot. We do this alongside the rest of a listing-prep exterior clean.

Storefront and office glass still faces customers all winter. We clean commercial glass on a day that isn't freezing, and we can set up a recurring schedule. See our commercial cleaning page.
