By late September in the Miami Valley, the north wall is usually green and the roof is streaking black. Soft wash removes algae on siding and roofs. Soft wash now — before winter locks that stain in.

This is not a pressure-wash timing piece. Driveways and sidewalks still get pressure / concrete wash. This article is about the soft surfaces that get ruined when someone turns the PSI up instead of killing the growth.

Why fall is the soft-wash window

Ohio summers feed algae. Heat, humidity, shade — especially on north walls near the Great Miami and Mad River valleys. By September the growth is visible. By November the calendar is closing.

Below about 40°F, detergents lose punch and rinse water turns into ice on walks and ladders. We book exterior soft wash while the weather still cooperates — roughly April through November — and fall is the underrated slot. Spring is booked. Fall still has dates.

Waiting doesn’t “preserve” the stain. It lets living growth sit damp against vinyl, aluminum, painted wood, and asphalt shingles through freeze–thaw. Soft wash removes algae. Soft wash now — before winter locks that stain in.

Soft wash = siding and roofs (not concrete)

Hard rule, same on every job:

  • Siding and roofs → soft wash (low pressure + detergent that kills algae at the root).
  • Driveways, sidewalks, patios → pressure wash / concrete wash (force + surface cleaner).
  • Never soft-wash language on concrete. Never high pressure on vinyl or asphalt shingles.

Soft wash runs roughly 100–500 PSI at the surface — garden-hose force — with dwell time before rinse. Pressure washing runs 1,500–4,000 PSI and belongs on hard flatwork.

What winter does to untreated algae

Green film on siding and black roof streaks (Gloeocapsa magma) are living organisms. Pressure alone scrapes the top layer. Roots in the pores stay. They come back.

Through winter:

  1. Moisture hangs longer on shaded elevations.
  2. Freeze–thaw works organic stain into pores and chalked finishes.
  3. Spring pollen lands on last year’s leftover growth — so the house looks dirty faster.

A proper soft wash kills the organism. Typical hold on siding in this climate is about 12–18 months when the method is right. Roofs are a longer cycle after a real soft wash — not a pressure blast.

What we soft wash before the freeze

Vinyl and aluminum siding — algae and mildew in the shade bands. Soft wash lifts the film without driving water under the laps.

Painted wood and brick walls — force strips paint and chews mortar. Soft wash keeps pressure minimal.

Asphalt shingle roofs — black streaks are algae feeding on limestone filler. Soft wash only. High pressure strips UV granules and shortens roof life.

Eaves, soffits, and the dirty band under the gutters — same chemistry, same visit when it fits the ticket.

Need the driveway too? Same day is fine — soft wash the house/roof, pressure wash the concrete. Two methods. One crew. See service hubs for Dayton, Tipp City, and Fairborn.

How a fall soft-wash visit runs

  1. Protect — plants pre-wet and covered; fixtures and openings checked.
  2. Apply and dwell — biodegradable detergent at low pressure. Chemistry does the work.
  3. Rinse top down — controlled rinse; beds flushed again.
  4. Roof if needed — same soft-wash principle, never a power washer on shingles.

You don’t have to stand in the yard the whole time. Access to a spigot and the elevations we’re treating is enough. We send before-and-after so you can see it from the street.

Listing or property-manager note

Agents and PMs: curb appeal before photos or board walk-throughs is the same split — soft wash siding/roof, pressure wash walks and lots. Don’t let a one-method crew “save time” on vinyl. Snap a pic, get a surface-by-surface price, book before frost.
