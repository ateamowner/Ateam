Fall Window Cleaning in Dayton: Is It Worth Doing Now?

Yes. Fall is one of the two best times to clean exterior windows in the Dayton area. Here's what we clean, why hard water spots show up, and how to get a price.

The short answer

Yes. Fall is one of the two best times to clean exterior windows in the Dayton area, and spring is the other. Pollen and bug season is winding down, sprinklers are off, and you get clear glass for the holidays, listing photos or a storefront before the shopping rush. We clean exterior glass, screens and tracks, upstairs included. Inside glass is available when you ask for it.

I'm Anthony. I run A Team Contracting out of Tipp City, and window cleaning is its own service for us, not an afterthought. Here's how I'd think about it this time of year.

Why fall is a good time to clean windows

By late October, most of what makes windows dirty in the summer is over. The pollen is down. The bugs are thinning out. Sprinklers are getting shut off. That means glass you clean now tends to stay clean longer than glass you clean in July.

It's also when you look at your windows the most. The sun sits lower, so it hits the glass at an angle and shows every streak and spot. And with the holidays coming, you're about to have people over.

Our standard advice is twice a year, spring and fall. If you only do it once, fall is a good pick. We can keep cleaning windows through fall and into winter as long as it isn't freezing outside.

Hard water spots: why Dayton-area windows get them

If you have white, cloudy spots on the lower windows, that's usually from sprinklers or the hose. The water dries and leaves its minerals behind on the glass.

Our area's ground water is hard. The City of Dayton says it softens "extremely hard well water" at its plants, and its 2025 summary shows the raw well water at over 300 mg/L of hardness before treatment. Even after softening, the tap water averaged about 139 mg/L. When that water dries on glass, the minerals stay behind.

Light spots come off with a normal cleaning. Heavy buildup from years of sprinklers hitting the same windows can take a special treatment. We look at it when we're there and tell you straight what will come off and what won't.

Don't pressure wash your windows

This one comes up a lot. A pressure washer is the wrong tool for glass. Andersen, one of the big window makers, lists "power washers or pressurized cleaning tools" with the things that can damage its glass and frames.

How we keep it straight:

  • Windows: glass tools and a purified-water finish, never a pressure tip. Screens and tracks too.
  • Siding and roofs: soft wash, low pressure.
  • Driveways, sidewalks, patios: pressure wash / concrete wash.

What a fall window cleaning includes

  • Exterior glass, first floor and upstairs.
  • Sliding doors and French doors, same as the windows.
  • Screens, cleaned separately so they stop blocking light.
  • Tracks and frames, wiped out so windows slide and seal.
  • A walkthrough before we leave. If something isn't right, we fix it on the spot.

Standard service is outside only. Inside glass is available when you request it, just tell us when you book. For exterior-only jobs you don't need to be home, and we'll send before and after photos when we're done.

Fall is already when people book their gutter cleaning, and our Dayton gutter service covers the downspouts too. If we're on your property anyway, adding windows costs a lot less than a separate trip, because the truck and crew are already there.

Realtors and sellers: clean glass shows in the photos

Dirty windows show up in listing photos, especially on a sunny day or a front shot that faces the street. If a house is going on the market this fall, get the windows done before the photographer comes, not after. We do this alongside the rest of a listing-prep exterior clean.

Storefronts, offices and property managers

Your front glass is the first thing a customer sees. With holiday shopping starting after Thanksgiving on November 26, late October and early November is a good time to get storefront windows clean.

We handle storefronts, offices and retail spaces, and we can set up recurring service so you're not calling every time. Property managers and HOAs can bundle windows with walkway and building washes on the same visit. See our commercial cleaning page.
