What We Soft Wash

Softwash is the right tool for anything that can't handle high pressure. We use it on every surface that would be damaged or voided by power washing.

🏠

Vinyl & Aluminum Siding

Mold, algae, and mildew grow into siding panels and stay there. Softwash kills it at the source and leaves the surface clean without lifting paint or cracking panels.

🏔

Roofs & Shingles

Black streaks and moss come off with low pressure, never a power washer. Roofs are their own craft — see our roof cleaning page for the full detail.

🏖

Eaves & Soffits

The underside of your roofline traps moisture and grows mold fast. We softwash eaves and soffits as part of a full exterior treatment or as a standalone job.

🌏

Stucco & EIFS

High pressure and stucco don't mix. Soft washing removes surface contamination safely, restoring color and protecting the finish from further damage.

🌖

Wood Siding & Cedar

Wood is sensitive. Too much pressure causes grain raise, splintering, and moisture intrusion. Softwash gets wood clean without destroying the surface underneath.

🎉

Fences & Painted Surfaces

Painted fences, decorative trim, and finished wood get cleaned safely with low-pressure application — no peeling, no blasting, no damage.

Yes — it's actually safer than pressure washing. The cleaning solution does the work, not water pressure. We apply it at garden-hose pressure and it won't lift paint, crack panels, or force water behind your siding. Most siding manufacturers actually recommend soft washing over power washing for this reason.

We use a professional-grade sodium hypochlorite solution (similar to bleach, but diluted and applied correctly) combined with surfactants and a neutralizer. It's biodegradable, and we pre-wet all landscaping before applying, use appropriate dilutions for each surface, and rinse thoroughly afterward. I've done hundreds of jobs without plant damage.

3–5 years is typical for siding. That's 2–3 times longer than pressure washing alone, because softwash kills the mold and algae at the root rather than just blasting the surface. When the surface is treated properly, there's nothing alive left to regrow quickly.

Yes — and soft washing is the only safe way to clean asphalt shingles. The black streaks you see are Gloeocapsa magma algae.
