By Surface

How We Clean Each One

Driveways

A rotary surface cleaner covers the slab in even passes, so you don't get the zebra stripes a hand wand leaves.

Front walks

Curved walks and steps get the surface cleaner where it fits and careful wand work on the edges and risers.

Paver patios

Pavers get cleaned without blasting out the joints, including around built-ins like the outdoor kitchen in the photos.

Oil and rust spots

Oil that has soaked in needs a degreaser and time, not more pressure. Rust needs a different treatment again. We'll tell you on site what will come out and what will only lighten.
