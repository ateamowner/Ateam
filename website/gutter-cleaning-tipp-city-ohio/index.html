What Gutter Cleaning Includes

A real gutter cleaning is more than blowing leaves out of the trough. Here's everything that's part of the job.

🍂

Gutter Troughs

Leaves, shingle grit, seed pods, and the packed sludge at the bottom — scooped out by hand so the trough is actually empty, not just rearranged.

🚿

Downspouts Flushed

A clear trough means nothing if the downspout is packed at the elbow. We flush each one and clear blockages until water runs free at the bottom.

🗑️

Debris Hauled Away

We bag what comes out and take it with us. You shouldn't be left with piles of wet gutter muck on the lawn or in your own bin.

💧

Flow Test Before We Leave

We run water through the system and watch it drain. If something still backs up, we're still on the ladder — not already down the road.

🧼

Gutter Face Brightening

Those black vertical stripes on the outside of the gutter aren't dirt that rinses off. Ask about adding a face cleaning while we're already set up.

👀

Roofline & Soffit Check

While we're up there we look. Loose spikes, sagging runs, separated seams — we tell you what we see so nothing surprises you later.

What Drives the Price of a Gutter Cleaning

Gutter cleaning starts at $99. Where your job lands depends on the factors below — send a photo through the estimator and we'll give you the exact number before we start.

Linear feet of gutter

A ranch with 120 feet is a different job than a wraparound two-story. Roof footprint is the biggest single factor.

Single story or two

Height changes the ladder work and the time. Two-story runs and steep approaches take longer to do safely.

How long it's been

First cleaning in three years with maples overhead is a heavier job than a well-maintained annual visit.

Number of downspouts

Every downspout is its own flush and its own potential clog at the elbow.

Gutter guards

Guards that have to come off and go back on add time. Some types are quick; some are not.

Add-ons while we're set up

Gutter face brightening or a roof soft wash on the same visit costs less than booking it as a separate trip.
