The Method

Soft Wash and Pressure Wash Are Not the Same Job

People search pressure washers near me and get a rental. The machine is the easy part. The mistake is using it on the wrong surface. If you are in the Miami Valley, skip the rental — we bring the right method for the material.

Soft washing is for siding and roofs. Low pressure, a cleaning solution, and a rinse. Vinyl, painted wood, and asphalt shingles live here. A pressure washer on those surfaces cracks panels, drives water behind them, and strips the granules off a roof.

Pressure washing — the same work people call power washing — is for driveways, sidewalks, patios, and other hardscape. Concrete gets a surface cleaner so the slab does not end up striped. Soft washing is not a concrete service. A driveway quote that uses the siding method is the wrong tool.

The longer version, with a side-by-side, is soft wash vs pressure wash. Questions that are not about a specific town are on the FAQ.
