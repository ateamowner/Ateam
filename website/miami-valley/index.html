The Method

Soft Wash, Pressure Wash & Windows — Three Jobs, One Crew

We pick the tool by the surface. Wrong method damages the house or leaves a weak concrete clean.

Soft wash — siding, roofs, brick & stone

House washing in the Miami Valley is a soft wash, not a pressure-wand job. Green algae, black mildew, and roof streaks live in the surface — blasting them with high force drives water behind vinyl, strips paint, and can chew granules off shingles. We use low pressure plus a biodegradable cleaning solution so the chemistry does the work: vinyl, aluminum, and painted siding; asphalt-shingle roofs (soft wash only); brick and stone walls where full pressure would damage the joint or film.

House washing in Dayton · Soft washing in Tipp City · Soft wash vs. pressure wash

Pressure / concrete — driveways, walks, patios

Concrete is a different job. Driveways, sidewalks, patios, and garage floors get pressure washing (same work people call power washing) with a surface cleaner so the slab finishes even — not zebra stripes from a wand. We do not soft-wash concrete as the primary concrete method. Soft wash is for walls and roofs. Pressure + surface cleaner is for hard flatwork.

Windows (+ gutters)

Window cleaning is often the easy yes — exterior glass, screens and tracks as scoped, upstairs included when it's on the ticket. A lot of Miami Valley homeowners add a soft wash or driveway pressure wash while we're already there and save versus booking separately. Gutters: cleared by hand, downspouts flushed, debris hauled off, water run through before we leave.
