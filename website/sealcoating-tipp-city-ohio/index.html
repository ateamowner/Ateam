Winter Hits the Pad. Shade Hits the Roof.

Tipp City asphalt takes freeze-thaw like the rest of Miami County. The fix is not a thicker coat on a dirty driveway. The pad gets cleaned — we are already set up for that on a Tipp pressure / power-wash day — then sealcoated when it is dry.

The roof is a different conversation. Tipp north slopes hold moisture. After a Tipp roof wash, the clear sealcoat is the extra 5–10 years. We will not sell that coat on a streaked roof.

This page is Tipp only. We did not spin up Huber, Fairborn, Beavercreek or Vandalia sealcoat URLs — those cities do not have city×service pages for this work the way they do for soft wash, pressure wash and windows.
