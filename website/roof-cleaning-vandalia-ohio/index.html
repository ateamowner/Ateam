Local Knowledge

The Streaking Is Geography, Not Housekeeping

Low ground between two rivers holds damp air longer than the higher ground on either side. Damp air is half of what the roof algae needs. Shade is the other half. Same shingle, same age: a Vandalia north slope weathers faster than one on a ridge.

On the older ranch belt that is a long, low roof with years of it. On the newer estates off South Brown School Road and out toward the airport, the houses went up together, so the first streaking often shows on a whole cul-de-sac at once — even with no canopy yet, because the north wall still never sees sun.

The fix is the same as Tipp City: low-pressure chemistry, plants covered, granules left alone. If you also want the driveway, that is pressure / power washing — a different machine, a different page.

Vandalia is a short run down I-75 from the shop. No travel surcharge.
