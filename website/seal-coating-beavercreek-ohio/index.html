Local Knowledge

Beavercreek's Decorative Concrete Is a Different Case

Because Beavercreek grew subdivision by subdivision rather than outward from one center, its driveways and patios span several distinct concrete generations — and its newer developments favor stamped, colored and exposed-aggregate finishes far more than the plain broom finish common elsewhere in our service area.

Why decorative concrete fades without a sealer

Stamped and colored concrete gets its look from integral color or a surface stain, and both fade under UV exposure and wear faster than plain concrete without a protective topcoat. An unsealed stamped patio can visibly dull within a few seasons; a sealed one holds its color much longer.

Clear means clear — no color change

The acrylic sealer we use is clear, not tinted, so it won't alter the color of stamped or colored concrete. It adds a protective, slightly enhanced sheen on top of the existing finish rather than changing it.

Older subdivisions still benefit, just for different reasons

If your section of Beavercreek has plain gray concrete from an earlier build phase, the driveway-protection case for sealing (water, freeze-thaw, salt) applies the same way it does everywhere else — the color-preservation angle is specific to the newer stamped and decorative work.
