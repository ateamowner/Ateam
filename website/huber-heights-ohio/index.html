The Whole City, Both Sides of I-70

Huber Heights is the easiest town in the Miami Valley for us to work, and it is because of how it was built. Charles Huber's company put up more than ten thousand houses here between 1956 and 1992 to a handful of plans, so streets repeat. We can quote several houses off one walk-around and get through them in a day.

That is why the neighbor rate is better here than anywhere else we go. If three or four of you on the same road want the same week, ask — it is genuinely cheaper for everyone, and it is not a sales line, it is just how the work packs.

Nearby we are just as regular in Vandalia, Troy, Tipp City and Dayton.
