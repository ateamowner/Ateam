Why It Matters

The Cheapest Marketing on Your P&L

Nobody has ever chosen a restaurant because the sidewalk was clean. Plenty of people have driven past one because it wasn't. A black-stained entry, a greasy drive-thru lane and a dumpster corral you can smell from the parking spaces all say the same thing about how a business is run, and they say it before anyone reads your sign.

Commercial exterior cleaning is also the rare line item where deferring it costs more. Ground-in grease bonds harder every month. Leaf tannin etches into concrete over a season. Organic growth on a north-facing wall gets roots into the substrate. A quarterly maintenance visit is a fraction of what a rescue job costs after three years of nothing.

Property managers and multi-site operators

If you're running several locations across the Miami Valley, we'll price them as a portfolio and run them on a rotating schedule with consistent documentation for each site. One point of contact, one invoice format, photos attached to every visit.
