Straight Answers

What Comes Out and What Doesn't

Algae and dirt

The green and black on a shaded driveway is biological. It comes off completely, and that's most of what you see in the photo above.

Tire marks and grime

Road film and tire marks come off with the surface cleaner and a rinse.

Oil stains

Oil that has soaked into the concrete needs a degreaser and time, not more pressure. Old stains usually lighten a lot but may not disappear, and we'll tell you that before we start.

Flaking or pitted concrete

If the top layer is already flaking, washing won't put it back. We'll point it out on site so nobody is surprised.
