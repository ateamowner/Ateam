Local Knowledge

Pavers, Pool Decks and Old Stone

A lot of Centerville work is hardscape. Paver patios, stamped and stone pool decks, outdoor kitchens and long curved front walks. Pavers have to be cleaned without blasting the sand out of the joints, and natural stone needs less pressure than poured concrete, so the settings change as we move across the yard.

Uptown Centerville, around Main Street and Franklin Street, has some of the oldest stone buildings in the area, and plenty of newer Washington Township homes use stone or brick on the front elevation. Stone, brick and mortar get a low-pressure soft wash, not a wand. Siding on the back and sides gets the same soft wash, with the plants covered first.

Nearby we also work Kettering, Springboro, Miamisburg, Beavercreek and Dayton.
