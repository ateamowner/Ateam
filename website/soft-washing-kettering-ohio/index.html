What We Wash

Soft Wash Surfaces on a Kettering House

Vinyl siding

The green and gray film on vinyl is alive. A soft wash kills it instead of just knocking the top off, so the wall stays clean longer.

Trim, soffits and fascia

The underside of the roofline holds moisture and grows mildew fast. It gets the same low-pressure wash as the walls.

Gutter faces

The outside of the gutters picks up dark streaks. Those get washed with the house. Clogged gutters are a separate job: gutter cleaning around Dayton.

Front doors
