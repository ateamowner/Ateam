# Photos needed — Troy

The three Troy pages are live with real photos from the existing gallery, so
nothing is broken and nothing is a grey placeholder box. But those photos are
**not known to be from Troy**, and the alt text on the new pages says
"Miami County, Ohio" rather than "Troy, Ohio" for exactly that reason.

Swapping in genuinely local shots is worth doing. Google reads image alt text,
and a photo captioned with the town it was actually taken in is a real local
signal. Guessing at the town is not, and it is the kind of small dishonesty
that is not worth the ranking.

before/after pairing and getting the whole surface in frame. Tell me the
street or neighborhood for each and I'll write the alt text properly.

Drop them in a folder or text them over. Name them however you like — I'll
rename to the site's convention (`gallery-troy-siding-1.webp` and so on),
